The family that brought the Tibetan knot to India

The Tibetan knot is the heart of what makes a Tibetan rug a Tibetan rug. It is tied around a rod rather than row by row around warps, which lets a weaver set the knot density and pile height with real control. Our founder, Shahid Ali Shah, brought the Tibetan knot to India, and the method he carried over still runs on our looms today. That is why our tibetan carpets read as the real thing: the same construction that gives these rugs their weight, their drape and their slow, even wear.

We keep the technique honest. A Tibetan rug from our looms is hand-knotted, not printed or glued, so the pattern lives in the pile and not on top of it.

What we make, and how

Most of our tibetan wool rugs are hand-knotted from hand-carded highland wool, chosen for its luster, spring and long life underfoot. We can also blend in silk or run higher knot counts where a fine design calls for it. Pile height, knot density, pattern and palette are all yours to set.

Every custom order runs the same way, so there are no surprises on the loom:

  1. You send a size, a reference or a render, and a palette.
  2. We return a strike-off, a woven sample in your colors and quality, for sign-off.
  3. Once you approve it, we weave on our own looms in Nepal or India.
  4. We inspect, wash, finish and ship.

Because we run the looms, the strike-off you approve is the standard the finished rug is held to, with no middle layer in between.

What to look for in a Tibetan rug

A short checklist worth running before you order tibetan rugs from anyone.

A real Tibetan knot

Ask whether the rug is hand-knotted with the Tibetan knot. Printed or tufted copies will not wear or drape the same way.

Named wool

Ask which wool and where it is from. Highland wool brings the luster and spring that give a Tibetan rug its life.

A strike-off

Insist on an approved woven sample in your colors before production. Color on a screen is not color in wool.

Who holds the loom

Buying from the manufacturer means the quality you approve is the quality that ships, with no handoff in between.
