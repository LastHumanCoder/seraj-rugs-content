What we make for commercial spaces

Commercial rugs carry a different job than a single rug for a home. They have to repeat cleanly across many rooms, take steady foot traffic, and still read as one design when a property is finished. We weave for all of it.

Hospitality rugs

For lobbies, lounges and function rooms we build hospitality rugs in sizes and shapes that fit the architecture, including runners for corridors and large single pieces for open floors.

Hotel rugs across a property

When a spec calls for the same pattern in dozens of guest rooms, consistency is everything. We hold the color and construction across the full run so hotel rugs match room to room and floor to floor.

How we run a commercial program

Every project moves the same way, so a buyer knows what happens at each step.

  1. You send a project schedule: sizes, quantities, constructions, palette and a target window.
  2. We return a strike-off, a woven sample in your colors and quality, for sign-off.
  3. Once you approve it, we weave on our own looms in Nepal and India and route volume across both workshops as needed.
  4. We inspect, wash, finish and ship against the schedule.

Because we run the looms, your commercial rugs do not pass through a middle layer, and the strike-off you approve is the standard the finished order is held to.

What to check before you spec commercial rugs

A short checklist worth running before you commit a hospitality order to any maker.

Construction for the traffic

Ask which construction suits the space. A guest room and a lobby corridor do not need the same density or finishing.

Repeatability

For a property-wide run, confirm the maker can hold color and quality across many pieces, not the first one alone.

A strike-off

Insist on an approved woven sample in your colors before production. Color on a screen is not color in wool.

Who holds the loom

Buying from the manufacturer means the quality you approve is the quality that ships, with no handoff in between.

Frequently asked questions

Do you make commercial rugs for hotels and hospitality?+
Yes. Hospitality is our most active trade segment. We make commercial rugs for lobbies, corridors, lounges, function rooms and guest rooms, in sizes and constructions set to the project. We work directly with designers and hospitality buyers as the manufacturer.
Can you hold one design across a whole property?+
Yes. When a spec calls for the same pattern across many rooms, we hold the color and construction across the full run so hotel rugs match room to room and floor to floor. We confirm this on an approved strike-off before production.
Which construction is right for high-traffic areas?+
It depends on the space. Lobbies and corridors need density and finishing that take steady wear, while guest rooms allow more range. We match hand-knotted or hand-tufted construction to the traffic each area will see and set it out in the strike-off.
How do you handle a large commercial order?+
We run our own looms in Nepal and India, with roughly 200 looms and 500 weavers, so we can route a large run across both workshops to hold quality and lead time. Send a project schedule with sizes, quantities and a target window, and we build to it.
What is a strike-off, and why does it matter on a commercial job?+
A strike-off is a small woven sample made in your chosen colors, construction and quality. You approve it before full production, so a property-wide order of commercial rugs matches what you signed off on rather than a screen image.
Do you supply commercial rugs to the trade?+
Yes. We work with interior designers, importers and hospitality buyers as a manufacturer. Start by sending a project schedule through our trade program, and we will take it from strike-off to delivery.
