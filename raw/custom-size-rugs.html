What we can build to size

Size is the start of most of our trade work, not an exception to it. Give us a number and we weave to it.

Custom area rugs for a room

The most common request. We size custom area rugs to a seating plan, a dining table with chairs pulled out, or a bed with the right reveal on each side. You send the room dimension and the furniture footprint; we return a drawing and a strike-off.

Oversized single pieces

Our looms handle large single pieces, so a great room or a lobby does not need to be pieced together from smaller rugs. We weave the field in one run where the design allows.

Bespoke shapes

Round, oval, runner, octagon, or a shape matched to a curved wall or a stair. Bespoke rugs in nonstandard shapes are drawn to your template before they go on the loom.

How a custom order runs

Every custom order follows the same path, so there are no surprises once weaving starts.

  1. You send a size, a reference or a render, and a palette.
  2. We return a strike-off, a woven sample in your colors and quality, for sign-off.
  3. Once you approve it, we weave on our own looms in Nepal or India.
  4. We inspect, wash, finish and ship.

Because we run the looms, custom made rugs do not pass through a middle layer, and the strike-off you approve is the standard the finished rug is held to.

Why designers order custom from the maker

With roughly 200 looms and 500 weavers across Nepal and India, we hold real capacity, so a custom size is a routine order rather than a special favor. We route each piece to the country and construction that fit it best. Partners work with us directly, so a question about a knot count, a dye lot or a dimension reaches the people on the loom, not a reseller. For interior designers running several rooms at once, that means one maker for the whole set of custom area rugs on a plan.

What to confirm before you order custom size rugs

A short checklist worth running before you commission custom size rugs from anyone.

A real strike-off

Insist on an approved woven sample in your size, colors and quality before production. Color and scale on a screen are not color and scale in wool.

Who holds the loom

Ordering from the manufacturer means the size and quality you approve are what ships, with no handoff in between.

No size ceiling

Ask whether large or nonstandard pieces are woven in one run or pieced together. We weave single oversized fields where the design allows.

Honest construction

Knot count, pile height and finishing should be stated plainly, so the custom made rugs you specify are the ones you receive.

Frequently asked questions

What sizes can you make custom size rugs in?+
There is no fixed size list. We weave to your dimension, from a small accent piece to an oversized single field for a great room or lobby. Send the measurement and the furniture plan, and we draw and weave to it.
Can you make custom area rugs in a nonstandard shape?+
Yes. Round, oval, octagon, runner, or a shape matched to a curved wall or a stair landing. Bespoke rugs in nonstandard shapes are drawn to your template and approved as a strike-off before they go on the loom.
How do I order custom made rugs from you?+
Send a brief or a render with a size, a reference and a palette. We return a strike-off, a woven sample in your colors and quality, for sign-off. Once you approve it, we weave on our own looms.
What is a strike-off?+
A strike-off is a small woven sample made in your chosen size, colors and quality. You approve it before full production, so the finished rug matches what you signed off on rather than a screen image.
Do you work with interior designers on custom projects?+
Yes. We work with interior designers, importers and hospitality buyers as a manufacturer. For a single room you can start with a strike-off; for several rooms or a larger program, talk to us through our trade page.
Do you make custom size rugs in Nepal or India?+
Both. We run our own looms in Nepal and India and route each order to the country and construction that suit it, which gives our partners flexibility on lead time and logistics.
