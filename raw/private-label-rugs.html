What private label rugs mean when the maker runs the looms

A private label rug is a rug you sell under your own name that someone else weaves to your spec. The question that decides quality is who that someone is. We are the manufacturer, with our own looms in Nepal and India, so your program does not pass through a trading desk that marks up a rug it never touched.

That means the people who answer a question about a knot count, a color shift or a finishing detail are the same people running the loom. You get a direct line to production, and the sample you approve is the one held against the finished goods.

How a private label program runs with us

Every program runs the same way, whether you start from our library or your own artwork.

  1. You send a design or pick one from our library, with sizes, quality and a palette.
  2. We return a strike-off, a woven sample in your colors and construction, for sign-off.
  3. You confirm label, tag and packaging details so your branding is built in, not bolted on.
  4. We weave on our own looms, inspect, wash, finish and ship under your name.

Because we hold the looms, the strike-off you approve is the standard your private label rugs are measured against on the floor.

Why partners private label with the manufacturer

With roughly 200 looms and 500 weavers across Nepal and India, we hold real capacity and can scale a label program without handing it to a third party. Partners work with us directly, so pricing, lead time and quality are settled with the maker rather than relayed through a reseller. If you need a larger or ongoing program, our trade program is built for exactly that kind of relationship.

What to confirm before you private label rugs

A short checklist worth running with any private label rug supplier.

Who holds the loom

A true oem rug manufacturer owns production. Ask whether your supplier weaves the rugs or buys them in, because that decides who stands behind the quality.

A strike-off first

Insist on an approved woven sample in your colors and quality before a program runs. A screen image is not a woven rug.

Branding built in

Confirm labels, care and content tags, and packaging up front so your name is part of the order, not an afterthought.

Design ownership

Your artwork stays yours. Agree in writing that designs you send are woven for you and not resold to others.

Frequently asked questions

What are private label rugs?+
Private label rugs are rugs you sell under your own brand that are woven to your spec by a manufacturer. With us, that manufacturer is the one running the looms, so the rugs carry your name while we handle production from fiber to finish.
Are you an OEM rug manufacturer?+
Yes. We are an oem rug manufacturer with our own looms in Nepal and India. We weave to your design and quality and finish the goods with your label, so what ships is yours end to end.
Can I use my own design, or do I pick from a library?+
Either. Send your own artwork, render or reference and we weave it to spec, or choose a pattern from our in-house design library and have it rewoven in your size, quality and palette.
Do you add our label and packaging?+
Yes. We add your woven or stitched label, your care and content tags, and pack to your spec so each rug arrives ready for your shelf or project. Confirm the details up front and we build them into the order.
What is the difference between white label rugs and private label rugs?+
In practice we treat them the same here: both are rugs we weave for you to sell under your own name with no mark of ours. White label rugs usually start from an existing design, while private label programs often involve your own artwork, but either way you own the finished product.
Can you handle a larger or ongoing program?+
Yes. With roughly 200 looms and 500 weavers across Nepal and India we hold real capacity, and our trade program is set up for repeat and larger-volume partners. Start with a strike-off, or talk to us about a program through our trade page.
