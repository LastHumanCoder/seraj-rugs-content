How a wholesale order runs

Every program follows the same path, so there are no surprises on the loom or at the dock.

  1. You send sizes, quantities, a reference or render, and a palette.
  2. We return a strike-off, a woven sample in your colors and quality, for sign-off.
  3. Once you approve it, we route the run to Nepal or India and weave on our own looms.
  4. We inspect, wash, finish and ship, with the approved strike-off as the standard the run is held to.

Because we are the maker, a question about a knot count, a color shift or a delivery window reaches the people who can answer it, not a reseller.

What to confirm before you place a bulk rug order

A short checklist worth running with any wholesale supplier.

Who holds the loom

A true manufacturer owns the weaving. Ask whether the capacity you are quoted is in-house or bought in from a third party.

A strike-off per run

Insist on an approved woven sample in your colors and quality before production, so repeat orders match rather than drift.

Routing and lead time

Ask whether the supplier can place a run in more than one country, which gives you options on lead time and logistics.

Quality control

Confirm how each run is inspected, washed and finished, and what the approved standard is measured against.
