A room-by-room rug size guide

Living room

Measure your seating group, then pick a rug that sits under at least the front legs of every piece. In most living rooms that means 8x10 or 9x12. A rug that floats in the middle, touching nothing, makes a room feel smaller.

Dining room

Add 24 inches to each side of your table so chairs stay on the rug when pulled out. A table for six usually wants an 8x10; a table for eight, a 9x12.

Bedroom

For a queen or king, an 8x10 under the lower two-thirds of the bed leaves a soft border on three sides. If you want a smaller footprint, a pair of runners along each side also works. This is the heart of the what size rug for bedroom question: frame the bed, do not hide it.

Rug placement rules that matter more than the size

Good rug placement forgives a size that is slightly off; bad placement spoils a size that is perfect.

  • Leave an even border of floor around the rug, usually 8 to 18 inches, rather than running it wall to wall.
  • In a living room, keep the rug wide enough to anchor the sofa and the chairs as one group.
  • In open-plan spaces, use rugs to define zones, and keep the borders between them consistent.
  • Center the rug on the main feature of the room, the bed or the sofa, not on the room's geometry.

When to skip standard sizes and go custom

Standard rug sizes are a convenience, not a rule. A room that measures 11 feet across will always leave a 9x12 looking a touch mean and a 10x14 crowding the walls. Rather than settle, our partners send us the exact dimensions and we weave to them. There is no size penalty with a maker: a custom size costs the same effort to design as a standard one, and it is the difference between a rug that fits and a rug that is close.

Frequently asked questions

What are the most common standard rug sizes?+
The sizes most people treat as standard are 2x3, 3x5, 4x6, 5x8, 6x9, 8x10, 9x12 and 10x14 feet, plus runners around 2.5x8 for halls. The 5x8, 8x10 and 9x12 cover the majority of living and dining rooms.
What size rug do I need for a living room?+
Start from your seating group and choose a rug that sits under at least the front legs of every piece. For most living rooms that is an 8x10 or 9x12. The rug should tie the furniture together rather than float between the pieces.
What size rug fits under a dining table?+
Add about 24 inches to each side of the table so chairs stay on the rug when pulled out. A six-seat table usually needs an 8x10 and an eight-seat table a 9x12.
What size rug works in a bedroom?+
For a queen or king bed, an 8x10 placed under the lower two-thirds of the bed leaves a comfortable border on three sides. Side-by-side runners are a good alternative when you want a smaller rug footprint.
How much floor should show around a rug?+
An even border of roughly 8 to 18 inches of floor around the rug looks balanced in most rooms. Running a rug nearly wall to wall reads like broadloom carpet rather than an area rug.
Can I order a rug in a size that is not standard?+
Yes. We weave to any dimension, so if your room falls between two standard sizes you can order the exact size you need. A custom size is no harder for us to make than a standard one; send the measurements and we will quote and sample it.
