What hand tufting is, and when it is the right call

Hand tufting punches yarn through a backing cloth stretched on a frame, following a design drawn straight onto that cloth. The pile is then sheared, a second backing and binding are added, and the rug is washed and finished. It is a hand process, just a different one from knotting, and it suits the jobs where you need a precise custom look without the longer timeline of a hand-knotted piece.

Hand tufted wool rugs

Wool is our default pile for hand tufted wool rugs. It takes color cleanly, holds a carved or high-low surface well, and wears the way partners expect in a room that sees daily traffic.

How we run a hand tufted order

Every order runs the same way, so there are no surprises off the frame.

  1. You send a size, a reference or a render, and a palette.
  2. We return a sample in your colors and quality for sign-off.
  3. Once you approve it, we tuft, carve, wash and finish in our India workshop.
  4. We inspect, bind and ship.

Because we run the workshop, the order does not pass through a middle layer, and the sample you approve is the standard the finished rug is held to.

What to look for in a hand tufted rug supplier

A short checklist worth running before you order hand tufted rugs from anyone.

Who holds the workshop

Buying from the manufacturer means the quality you approve is the quality that ships, with no handoff in between.

A sample first

Insist on an approved sample in your colors and pile before a bulk run. Color and texture on a screen are not the real thing.

Real capacity

Ask for honest output figures and lead times. A supplier should be able to tell you what it can run and by when.

Named materials

Ask which wool and which backing. Pile fiber, pile height and finishing should be stated plainly, not dressed up.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between hand tufted and hand knotted rugs?+
Hand knotting ties each knot by hand onto the warp and takes much longer, which suits heirloom pieces. Hand tufting punches yarn through a backing cloth that carries the design, then adds a second backing and binding. Hand tufted rugs reach a sharp custom look faster and at a lower effort per square meter, which makes them a strong fit for volume and for projects on a deadline.
